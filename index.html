"A refined Himalayan retreat where nature, comfort, and culture blend seamlessly. Experience Gangtok like a local — with elegance, safety, and heartfelt warmth."

Hosted by a doctor couple, Nabhasa Courtyard offers an elegant yet homely experience. Located near the famous Banjhakri Falls and Rumtek Monastery, our boutique homestay provides mountain-view rooms, family apartments, and an organic garden ambiance. Enjoy the rare assurance of a doctor on call in a peaceful hillside location.
